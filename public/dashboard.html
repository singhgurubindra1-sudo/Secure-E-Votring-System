A demonstration electoral services portal, built for academic review Here’s how you can tell
This is not a live election service

The electoral roll, the candidates and the ballots are sample data held on this server. No ballot cast here is reported to any election authority.

A secure session, even so

Sign-in is signed and time-limited, the session cookie is HTTP-only and same-site, and a sealed ballot is stored with no link back to the voter who cast it.

Constitution of India · Article 326

Right to Vote

Every citizen of eighteen years and over has one vote, of equal weight to every other vote cast. These services are how you use yours.

Electoral services

Four services, each one working from your voter ID number.

  • 01

    Check the electoral roll

    Search the published roll by name or voter ID and confirm your entry is correct before polling day.

    • Open to every signed-in voter
    • No documents needed
    Start now
  • 02

    Download your voter card

    Produce a print-ready PDF of your card, with your photograph where one is held on file.

    • Needs your name, date of birth and voter ID
    • Camera check where a photograph is on file
    Start now
  • 03

    Cast your ballot

    Confirm your details and seal a single ballot. Your choice is stored with no link back to you.

    • One ballot per voter ID, used once
    • Camera check where a photograph is on file
    Start now
  • 04

    Report a problem

    Raise a wrong entry, a missing name or anything else. Attach photographs or a PDF as evidence.

    • Reply sent to your registered email
    • Reference number issued at once
    Start now
What is published, and what is not

Names on the electoral roll are published so that anyone may verify it. Your ballot choice is not, and cannot be recovered from what is stored.
