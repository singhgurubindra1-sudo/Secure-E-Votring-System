This is not a live election service

The electoral roll, the candidates and the ballots are sample data held on this server. No ballot cast here is reported to any election authority.

A secure session, even so

Sign-in is signed and time-limited, the session cookie is HTTP-only and same-site, and a sealed ballot is stored with no link back to the voter who cast it.
