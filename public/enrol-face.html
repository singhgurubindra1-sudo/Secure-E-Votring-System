Enrol a face

Registers the reference face for one voter ID. The photographs are read in this browser; only the resulting face measurements and one cropped portrait are sent to the server.

Use two or three clear, front-facing photographs of the same person, each with only that person in frame. More angles make the live check more forgiving.
